## Prompt

```text
Use the attached pose sheet as the exact reference for this character: same face, same equipment, same colors, same proportions and SAME SIZE as the first (idle) drawing.

Character: veiled Almoravid light horseman, riding a horse. Design (team color = the team color below): Rider: brown skin, a team-color turban with one loose tail, and a deep indigo litham veil covering the mouth and nose (only the eyes show), a white wool burnous with team-color borders, brown leather boots, a small round white lamt shield of antelope hide with a team-color Amazigh geometric pattern (diamonds and the yaz sign, no writing), a spear with a dark shaft and a steel spearhead. Horse: slender sandy dun horse, cream mane and tail, black hooves, a simple brown bridle, a team-color saddle cloth with a cream fringe. Horse and rider are ONE drawing; the horse faces right.

Colors must match the in-game pixel-art sprites exactly. Skin: warm copper brown (#8E5A34, shadow #6A3F22) for every soldier. Hair: very dark brown (#4D1F0F), short and curly. Team color: crimson red (#D13A2E, shadow #8E1F24, light #F2735A), used ONLY on the team parts named in the design. Metal: gold (#D69625, light #FAC777). Cloth: cream (#EFD6A8). Wood: warm brown (#9F5021). No other red, crimson, pink or orange-red anywhere in the image (mouths and lips in dark brown), so the team color can be swapped automatically.

Create 4 frames of a hand-drawn animation sequence, side by side in one wide image, 4 equal invisible columns, as if filmed by a locked camera: the character stays at the same place in each column, on the same ground baseline, facing RIGHT in a 3/4 side view, same size in every frame. Nothing touches the image edges or the neighbouring frame. Every weapon is drawn whole and stays inside its own column, never cut by the image edge, never crossing into the neighbouring column. The spear is always gripped by a visible hand and never passes behind or through the shield: keep its whole shaft and spearhead visible above, below or in front of the shield.

Frames, left to right:

1. Thrown off: the rider flying backward off the saddle, arms flailing, the horse startled.

2. Landing: the rider sitting on the ground in front of the horse, legs stretched forward, his own headgear or hair messed up over one eye (do not add any headgear he does not wear); the horse stands calmly behind him.

3. Dazed: rider still sitting, upper body swaying to the left, dizzy half-closed eyes, silly dazed smile; the horse sniffs his head.

4. Dazed: same sitting position, upper body swaying to the right, same dizzy face; the horse barely moves.

The character is knocked out in a comical, non-violent way, like in a family animated film: no blood, no injury. Frames 3 and 4 must be almost identical except for the sway, so they can loop. Do not draw any stars, birds or symbols above the head.

Style: classic hand-drawn 2D feature animation, 1990s cel look. Simple, appealing shapes and a strong readable silhouette. Clean uniform ink outline in dark brown-black (#2B1B17), never pure black. Few interior lines, no muscle definition lines, no fabric wrinkles beyond one or two folds, no small ornaments. Flat color fills with ONE darker shadow tone, light coming from the top left, no gradients, no texture, no painterly rendering, no glow.

Plain solid pure white background (#FFFFFF), no checkerboard, no ground shadow, no ground line, no text, no labels, no frame, no cell borders.
```
