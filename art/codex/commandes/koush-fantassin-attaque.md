## Prompt

```text
Use the attached pose sheet as the exact reference for this character: same face, same equipment, same colors, same proportions and SAME SIZE as the first (idle) drawing.

Character: young Nubian spearman of the Kingdom of Kush. Design (team color = the team color below): Very dark brown skin. Short tightly curled black hair with a team-color cloth headband tied at the back, two short loose tails flying behind, and one small white feather. No helmet, no beard. Bare chest and arms, a thin gold bead necklace, gold armlets and bracers. White linen kilt with a team-color front panel hanging down the middle. Brown leather sandals. Tall oval cowhide shield: cream hide with large dark-brown irregular spots and a thin team-color rim. Spear with a dark wooden shaft and a gold-bronze leaf-shaped spearhead.

Colors must match the in-game pixel-art sprites exactly. Skin: warm copper brown (#7A4420, shadow #5A2E14) for every soldier. Hair: very dark brown (#4D1F0F), short and curly. Team color: crimson red (#D13A2E, shadow #8E1F24, light #F2735A), used ONLY on the team parts named in the design. Metal: gold (#D69625, light #FAC777). Cloth: cream (#EFD6A8). Wood: warm brown (#9F5021). No other red, crimson, pink or orange-red anywhere in the image (mouths and lips in dark brown), so the team color can be swapped automatically.

Create 4 frames of a hand-drawn animation sequence, side by side in one wide image, 4 equal invisible columns, as if filmed by a locked camera: the character stays at the same place in each column, on the same ground baseline, facing RIGHT in a 3/4 side view, same size in every frame. Nothing touches the image edges or the neighbouring frame. Every weapon is drawn whole and stays inside its own column, never cut by the image edge, never crossing into the neighbouring column. The spear is always gripped by a visible hand and never passes behind or through the shield: keep its whole shaft and spearhead visible above, below or in front of the shield.

Frames, left to right:

1. Anticipation: weight on the back leg, spear pulled back at shoulder height, shield tucked, body coiled, determined face. The back foot stays planted at the same spot in all 4 frames.

2. Motion smear frame, like in classic hand-drawn animation: body lunging forward, the arm and spear stretched into a fast elongated shape with a few speed lines in the same ink style.

3. Full extension: deep lunge, front knee bent, spear fully thrust forward, shouting, headband tails flying behind.

4. Follow-through: still extended but settling, spear tip lowering slightly.

Style: classic hand-drawn 2D feature animation, 1990s cel look. Simple, appealing shapes and a strong readable silhouette. Clean uniform ink outline in dark brown-black (#2B1B17), never pure black. Few interior lines, no muscle definition lines, no fabric wrinkles beyond one or two folds, no small ornaments. Flat color fills with ONE darker shadow tone, light coming from the top left, no gradients, no texture, no painterly rendering, no glow.

Plain solid pure white background (#FFFFFF), no checkerboard, no ground shadow, no ground line, no text, no labels, no frame, no cell borders.
```
