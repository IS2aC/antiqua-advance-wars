## Prompt

```text
The attached image is the in-game pixel-art sprite sheet of this unit for this people (drawn in the red team color). Use it as the exact reference for the design: same equipment, same headwear, same shield and weapon, same skin tone, same colors and where each color goes. Keep this people's own look: do not mix in equipment from other peoples. Redraw it as a large hand-drawn animation character with natural proportions (not chibi, not pixel art).

Character: Asante (Ashanti) war chief carried in a royal palanquin by two bearers. Design (team color = the team color below): A gold-and-wood palanquin carried on two long poles by two bearers, one in front and one behind, the poles resting on their shoulders. Above it, a large team-color ceremonial umbrella with a gold fringe and a gold finial. The chief sits in the palanquin: very dark brown skin, a gold-and-black crown band, heavy gold necklaces, a team-color kente toga with black and gold stripes over one shoulder, holding a spear with a gold spearhead. The palanquin side has a gold adinkra emblem on a team-color panel. The bearers: very dark brown skin, a gold headband, a team-color loincloth, bare chest. Palanquin, chief and bearers are ONE drawing, moving to the right.

Colors must match the in-game pixel-art sprites exactly. Skin: warm copper brown (#6A3A1C, shadow #4A2711) for every soldier. Hair: very dark brown (#4D1F0F), short and curly. Team color: crimson red (#D13A2E, shadow #8E1F24, light #F2735A), used ONLY on the team parts named in the design. Metal: gold (#D69625, light #FAC777). Cloth: cream (#EFD6A8). Wood: warm brown (#9F5021). No other red, crimson, pink or orange-red anywhere in the image (mouths and lips in dark brown), so the team color can be swapped automatically.

Create a 2D animation pose sheet: 4 full-body drawings of the SAME character side by side in one horizontal row, 4 equal invisible columns, same size in every drawing, feet resting on the same baseline, generous empty space around each figure, nothing touching the image edges or the neighbouring figure. All drawings face RIGHT, in a 3/4 side view. Every weapon is drawn whole and stays inside its own column, never cut by the image edge, never crossing into the neighbouring column. The spear is always gripped by a visible hand and never passes behind or through the shield: keep its whole shaft and spearhead visible above, below or in front of the shield.

Poses, left to right:

1. Idle: the two bearers stand still with the poles on their shoulders, the chief seated upright in the palanquin under the umbrella, spear held up.

2. Attack: the bearers lunge forward, the chief leans out of the palanquin and thrusts the spear forward.

3. Hit: the bearers stagger backward, the palanquin tilting, the chief thrown back, off balance.

4. Defeated: the bearers kneeling, the palanquin resting tilted on the ground, the chief slumped over its side, head lowered.

Style: classic hand-drawn 2D feature animation, 1990s cel look. Simple, appealing shapes and a strong readable silhouette. Clean uniform ink outline in dark brown-black (#2B1B17), never pure black. Few interior lines, no muscle definition lines, no fabric wrinkles beyond one or two folds, no small ornaments. Flat color fills with ONE darker shadow tone, light coming from the top left, no gradients, no texture, no painterly rendering, no glow.

Plain solid pure white background (#FFFFFF), no checkerboard, no ground shadow, no ground line, no text, no labels, no frame, no cell borders.
```
