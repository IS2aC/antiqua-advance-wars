# Commande : ashanti / bateau / attaque

- Référence à joindre : `art/codex/livraisons/peuples/ashanti/bateau/poses.png`
- Livrable : `art/codex/livraisons/peuples/ashanti/bateau/attaque.png`

## Prompt

```text
Use the attached pose sheet as the exact reference for this character: same face, same equipment, same colors, same proportions and SAME SIZE as the first (idle) drawing.

Character: Asante war canoe with its paddlers and archers. Design (team color = the team color below): A very long carved wooden dugout canoe seen from the side, with gold ornaments on the prow, a team-color kente canopy in the middle, and a crew of Asante warriors with very dark brown skin, gold headbands and team-color kente waist cloths: one paddler at the stern with a paddle, two archers in front holding bows. The hull bottom sits on an invisible straight waterline at the bottom of the drawing: draw NO water, no waves, no splash under it (the sea is in the background painting). Canoe and crew are ONE drawing; the prow points right.

Colors must match the in-game pixel-art sprites exactly. Skin: warm copper brown (#6A3A1C, shadow #4A2711) for every soldier. Hair: very dark brown (#4D1F0F), short and curly. Team color: crimson red (#D13A2E, shadow #8E1F24, light #F2735A), used ONLY on the team parts named in the design. Metal: gold (#D69625, light #FAC777). Cloth: cream (#EFD6A8). Wood: warm brown (#9F5021). No other red, crimson, pink or orange-red anywhere in the image (mouths and lips in dark brown), so the team color can be swapped automatically.

Create 4 frames of a hand-drawn animation sequence, side by side in one wide image, 4 equal invisible columns, as if filmed by a locked camera: the character stays at the same place in each column, on the same ground baseline, facing RIGHT in a 3/4 side view, same size in every frame. Nothing touches the image edges or the neighbouring frame. Every weapon is drawn whole and stays inside its own column, never cut by the image edge, never crossing into the neighbouring column. The spear is always gripped by a visible hand and never passes behind or through the shield: keep its whole shaft and spearhead visible above, below or in front of the shield.

Frames, left to right:

1. Draw: the two Asante archers drawing their bows, aiming upward to the right. The hull stays at the same spot in all 4 frames.

2. Release: the two Asante archers loosing the arrows, a few speed lines.

3. The arrows flying away upward to the right, bows still raised.

4. Follow-through: the two Asante archers reaching for new arrows, the ship steady.

Style: classic hand-drawn 2D feature animation, 1990s cel look. Simple, appealing shapes and a strong readable silhouette. Clean uniform ink outline in dark brown-black (#2B1B17), never pure black. Few interior lines, no muscle definition lines, no fabric wrinkles beyond one or two folds, no small ornaments. Flat color fills with ONE darker shadow tone, light coming from the top left, no gradients, no texture, no painterly rendering, no glow.

Plain solid pure white background (#FFFFFF), no checkerboard, no ground shadow, no ground line, no text, no labels, no frame, no cell borders.
```
